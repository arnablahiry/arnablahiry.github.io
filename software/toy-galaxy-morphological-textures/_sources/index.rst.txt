galaxy-textures documentation
=============================

``galaxy-textures`` is a small procedural image generator for synthetic
galaxy morphology experiments. It keeps each morphology in its own module and
exposes a single public entry point, :func:`galaxy_textures.make_galaxy`.

The generated images are intentionally stylized and are meant for teaching,
demo, and interpretability work rather than astrophysical analysis.
