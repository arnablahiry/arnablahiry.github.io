Your family's papers, kept like treasures.

Lararium is a private vault for passports, IDs, cards, certificates and logins. It reads your documents on the phone, keeps them encrypted, and backs them up to your own Google Drive. No ads, no tracking, no account on anyone else's server.

Android 8 or newer · updates from inside the app · named after the lararium, the Roman household shrine where a family kept what mattered.

Home

Everyone in the family, at a glance

Home shows the people in your group (each with their own Google account) and the Common vault everyone shares, with upcoming events underneath. Search looks through document names and the details read from them, so typing the last digits of a number finds the card.

  • Light and dark
  • Groups: family, friends, flatmates
  • Search inside documents
  • What's new, after every update
Vault

Each person's vault, as a stack of cards

Every person has their own vault, and their documents stack like cards, each in its own colours and artwork with the issuing country's flag: passports, ID cards, visas, permits, licences, degrees, insurance. Tap one to lift it, tap again to open it; hold and drag to reorder. Prefer a list or icons? One tap switches.

  • Pull past the top to fan the cards
  • Country filters (IN · GR · USA…)
  • Cards, list or icons
  • Hold and drag to reorder
Documents

Scan it once, find it forever

Add a photo or PDF (several pages are fine) and Lararium reads it on the phone itself: names, numbers and dates (issued, valid until, birth) turn into fields you can copy with one tap, and any date can be picked on a calendar. Nothing is sent anywhere to be read. Open the file full screen to zoom, download it, or share it straight to another app.

  • On-device text reading
  • Passport MRZ, Aadhaar, PAN, visas, permits…
  • Issue and expiry dates, with a calendar
  • Multi-page, smooth pinch-zoom
  • Download & share
Events

Trips with their papers attached

Make an event for a trip or an appointment, say who's going and who can see it, and pin the documents it needs straight from people's vaults. Tickets and bookings live with the event. Notes take WhatsApp-style formatting, so a pasted itinerary keeps its bold and italics.

  • Pin documents from vaults
  • Per-person visibility
  • Reminders
  • *bold* _italic_ ~strike~ in notes
Passwords

Logins, behind a second PIN

Keep the logins that go with your documents (tax portals, DigiLocker, the bank). Each is a card; tap to lift it and View, Copy or Delete appear. Seeing or copying a password asks for a separate passwords PIN, and deleting needs you to type DELETE. Other members' logins never leave their phones; Common logins are sealed to each member's phone.

  • Separate passwords PIN
  • View, Copy, Delete on each card
  • Typed DELETE to remove
  • Never shared except Common
Groups

Share with the people who matter

A group is shared between phones with no server at all: each phone keeps an encrypted copy in its own Google Drive and reads the others'. To bring someone in, send them your invite link, privately (WhatsApp is fine): tapping it opens Lararium and joins. They send theirs back the same way, and until everyone has, Lararium shows who you're not synced with yet. After that, changes reach everyone within moments, with a quiet notification saying who changed what. Whoever made the group is its admin and can make others admins, help fix someone's documents, or remove a member.

  • No server at all: your Drives, encrypted
  • Invite links: one tap to join
  • Who you're synced with, at a glance
  • Silent “who changed what” notifications
  • Admins and members
Security & backup

Locked on the phone, sealed in your Drive

The vault is an encrypted database behind your passcode or fingerprint, and it locks itself when you leave. Backups go to your own Google Drive, encrypted, organised in one folder per person and per document, and only changed files are uploaded again. An optional backup password seals your logins a second time with a key only you know.

  • Passcode + fingerprint
  • Encrypted Drive backup
  • Optional backup password for logins
  • Screenshots blocked
Getting started

Two minutes to set up

Create an account with your Google account (that's where backups go), pick a picture, choose a passcode, and you're in. On a new phone, sign in with the same Google account and everything comes back.

  • Sign in with Google
  • No phone number
  • Restore on a new phone
Privacy

Yours, and only yours

Lararium has no server of its own. Your vault lives on your phone, your backups live in your Google Drive, and the app only ever sees the files it made there.

On the phone

  • An encrypted database (SQLCipher) and encrypted files, with keys in the Android Keystore.
  • Passcode or fingerprint to open; locks itself when you leave; screenshots blocked.
  • Documents are read by on-device text recognition; nothing is uploaded to be read.
  • A separate passwords PIN before any login is shown or copied.

In your Google Drive

  • Everything sealed with AES-256-GCM; names are readable, contents are not.
  • The app's Drive access is limited to files it created (drive.file) and its hidden app folder.
  • Optional backup password: logins are sealed again with a key only you know.
  • Groups share encrypted copies; Common logins are sealed to each member's phone.
  • No other server: joining, sharing and syncing go only through your family's own Drives.

Get Lararium

Free, for Android 8 and newer. The button always gets the newest version (release notes and older versions).

  1. Open the download on your phone and tap the .apk file.
  2. Allow Android to install apps from your browser or files app (asked once).
  3. From then on, Lararium updates itself from Settings › About.
